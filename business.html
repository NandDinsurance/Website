Questions

Business insurance questions

Do I need a business owner's policy?+

Many small businesses do. It combines liability and property coverage in one policy and is often cheaper than buying them separately.

Is workers' comp required in Texas?+

Texas doesn't require most private employers to carry it, but going without it has real legal and financial risks. We'll explain your options.

Can you send a certificate of insurance?+

Yes. Contact our office with the details and we'll get a certificate to you or your client.

Does my personal auto policy cover my work truck?+

Often not, if the vehicle is used mainly for business. Let's review how you use it so you're covered.
