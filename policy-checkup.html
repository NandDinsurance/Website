Questions

Checkup questions

Is the checkup really free?+

Yes. There's no cost and no obligation to buy or switch anything.

Do I have to be with your agency?+

No. We'll review a policy from any insurance company.

What's a declarations page?+

It's the summary page of your policy that lists your coverages, limits, deductibles and premium. A photo or PDF works.

Will you try to make me switch?+

Only if switching truly makes sense for you. Sometimes the best answer is a small change to the policy you already have.
