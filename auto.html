Piece by piece

What's inside an auto policy

Every policy is a mix of these coverages. Knowing what each one does makes it easier to choose the right limits.

01

Liability

Pays for injuries and damage you cause to other people. Texas requires it, and we'll help you choose limits that truly protect your savings.

02

Collision

Repairs or replaces your car after an accident, no matter who was at fault.

03

Comprehensive

Covers theft, vandalism, fire, falling objects, and North Texas hail damage.

04

Uninsured motorist

Protects you when the other driver has no insurance, or not enough of it.

05

Personal injury protection

Helps pay medical bills and lost wages for you and your passengers.

06

Medical payments (MedPay)

Helps pay medical and funeral costs for you and your passengers after an accident, no matter who was at fault.

07

Roadside & rental

Towing, jump-starts, lockouts, and a rental car while yours is in the shop.

Questions

Auto insurance questions

What do I need to get an auto quote?+

Each driver's name, date of birth and license number, each vehicle's year, make and model or VIN, and your current policy's declarations page if you have one.

Is the Texas minimum enough?+

The state minimum keeps you legal, but serious accidents often cost more than those limits. We'll show you what higher limits cost so you can decide.

Will a claim raise my rate?+

It depends on the claim and who was at fault. Call us before you file and we'll explain what to expect.

Can I add my teen driver?+

Yes. We'll go over the best way to add them and any options that may help keep the cost down.
