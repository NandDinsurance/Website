Questions

Life insurance questions

How much life insurance do I need?+

A common starting point is enough to replace your income for several years, pay off debts like your mortgage, and cover future costs like college. We'll work through the numbers with you.

Do I need a medical exam?+

Some policies require one and some don't. We'll tell you what to expect for the options you're considering.

Can I get coverage with a health condition?+

Often, yes. Rates and options vary, so the best step is to talk with us about your situation.

Can I have more than one policy?+

Yes. Some families combine a term policy for the working years with a smaller permanent policy for lifelong needs.
