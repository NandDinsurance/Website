Your options

Life insurance, made simple

There are two main kinds of life insurance. We'll explain both and help you choose.

Term life

Coverage for a set number of years, like 10, 20 or 30. Usually the most affordable way to protect a young family.

Whole life

Permanent coverage that lasts your whole life and builds cash value over time.

Universal life

Permanent coverage with more flexibility in how much you pay and how much coverage you keep.

Mortgage protection

Coverage sized to pay off your home, so your family can stay in it.

Coverage for couples

Protect both partners' incomes and the work each one does for the family.

Business owners

Help protect your business and your family if an owner or key person passes away.

Questions

Life insurance questions

How much life insurance do I need?+

A common starting point is enough to replace your income for several years, pay off debts like your mortgage, and cover future costs like college. We'll work through the numbers with you.

Is term or whole life better?+

It depends on your goals. Term is usually best for affordable protection during your working years; permanent coverage fits longer-term needs. We'll explain the difference for your situation.

Do I need a medical exam?+

Some policies require one and some don't. We'll tell you what to expect for the options you're considering.

Can I get coverage if I have a health condition?+

Often, yes. Rates and options vary, so the best step is to talk with us about your situation.
